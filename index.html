Use your location?

Echoo uses your current location only to sort GTA places and plans by real distance. You can explore any GTA municipality without it.

iPhone (iOS) Directions:
  1. Open your iPhone Settings.
  2. Tap Privacy & Security > Location Services.
  3. Scroll and tap your browser.
  4. Select While Using the App and ensure Precise Location is on.
